# EPF Invest — Design System Specification

Measured design tokens contract derived from institutional financial analytics benchmark.

---

## 🎨 Color Ramps & Token System

### Surfaces (Theme-Invariant Depth Ramp)
- `--surface-page`: `#070814` (Deep obsidian backdrop)
- `--surface-panel`: `rgba(17, 18, 36, 0.65)` (Glassmorphic panel container)
- `--surface-raised`: `rgba(26, 27, 48, 0.75)` (Elevated card background)
- `--surface-hover`: `rgba(37, 38, 66, 0.85)` (Interactive hover state)
- `--surface-active`: `rgba(48, 49, 82, 0.95)` (Pressed / active selection state)

### Borders & Hairlines
- `--border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` (Card container hairlines)
- `--border-base`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` (Divider lines and input borders)
- `--border-strong`: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)` (Interactive focus ring & active bounds)

### Typography Ramp
- `--text-primary`: `#f1f3fd` (High contrast body & title headings)
- `--text-secondary`: `#a5a1b8` (Subtitles, labels, and table metadata)
- `--text-tertiary`: `#6c6785` (Muted hints, table headers, and axis labels)
- `--text-accent`: `#c4b5fd` (Violet highlighted metric values)

### Accent & Semantic Signals
- `--accent`: `#8b5cf6` (Institutional Violet primary accent)
- `--accent-hover`: `#7c3aed` (Primary button hover fill)
- `--accent-wash`: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)` (Badge & chip backgrounds)
- `--accent-glow`: `0 0 24px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)` (Glowing card elevation)
- `--semantic-success`: `#34d399` (Positive returns & buy acquisitions)
- `--semantic-danger`: `#f87171` (Negative returns & sell transactions)
- `--semantic-warning`: `#fbbf24` (Dividend & hold notifications)
